**MSO Automotive**, a leading automotive manufacturing company in
Malaysia, is undergoing a digital transformation to improve its
manufacturing processes through **High Performance Data Processing
(HPDP)** and **Big Data Analytics**.

The company deploys **sensors** and **Internet of Things (IoT)** devices
across its production lines to continuously collect real-time data,
including:

-   Equipment performance
-   Production rates
-   Quality metrics

The collected data is processed using advanced analytics to identify
production inefficiencies and support data-driven decision-making.

One major application is **predictive maintenance**, where sensor data
is analyzed to predict equipment failures before they occur. This
minimizes unexpected downtime, extends machine lifespan, and reduces
maintenance costs.

MSO Automotive also employs big data analytics for **quality control**
by analyzing data from raw material inspection, production processes,
and final product testing. Patterns associated with product defects are
identified, allowing corrective actions to be taken in real time.

In addition, the company optimizes its **supply chain** by analyzing
historical production data and demand forecasts to improve inventory
management, production scheduling, and supplier relationships.
